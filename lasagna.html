Lasagna


Lasagna is a comforting Italian-style baked pasta dish made with layers of tender pasta sheets, rich meat sauce, creamy cheese, and melted mozzarella. This simple version keeps the preparation easy while still delivering a hearty and flavorful meal that is perfect for family dinners or gatherings. Each layer combines savory beef, tomato sauce, and creamy cheeses into a warm, satisfying dish with a golden, bubbly top.

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 500 g (1 lb) ground beef
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 700 g (24 oz) tomato sauce
  • 1 tomato, sliced
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 250 g (1 cup) ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil

    • Steps

    1. Preheat the oven to 190°C (375°F).
    2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions, then drain and set aside.
    3. Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium heat and cook the onion until softened.
    4. Add the garlic and ground beef, cooking until the beef is browned.
    5. Stir in the tomato sauce, tomato paste, salt, pepper, and oregano, then simmer for 10 minutes.
    6. In a bowl, mix the ricotta cheese, egg, and half of the Parmesan cheese.
    7. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce in the bottom of a baking dish.
    8. Add a layer of noodles, followed by ricotta mixture, meat sauce, and mozzarella cheese.
    9. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used, finishing with mozzarella and the remaining Parmesan cheese on top.
    10. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes.
    11. Remove the foil and bake for another 10–15 minutes until the cheese is melted and golden.
    12. Let the lasagna rest for 10 minutes before slicing and serving.
