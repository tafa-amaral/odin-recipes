Steak


A simple steak recipe focuses on bringing out the natural flavor of the meat with minimal ingredients and straightforward cooking techniques. This pan-seared steak develops a rich, golden crust on the outside while staying juicy and tender on the inside. With just a few seasonings and a hot skillet, you can create a restaurant-style steak at home that pairs perfectly with potatoes, vegetables, or a fresh salad.

  • 2 beef steaks (ribeye, sirloin, or strip steak)
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 2 cloves garlic, smashed
  • 2 sprigs fresh rosemary or thyme (optional)

    • Steps

    1. Remove the steaks from the refrigerator about 20–30 minutes before cooking to bring them to room temperature.
    2. Pat the steaks dry with paper towels and season both sides with salt and black pepper.
    3. Heat a heavy skillet or cast-iron pan over high heat until very hot.
    4. Add the olive oil to the pan, then place the steaks in carefully.
    5. Cook the steaks for about 3–4 minutes on the first side without moving them to create a good crust.
    6. Flip the steaks and cook another 3–4 minutes for medium-rare, adjusting the time for your preferred doneness.
    7. Add the butter, garlic, and rosemary or thyme to the pan during the last minute of cooking.
    8. Spoon the melted butter over the steaks repeatedly to enhance flavor and tenderness.
    9. Remove the steaks from the pan and let them rest for 5 minutes before serving.
    10. Serve hot with your favorite side dishes.
