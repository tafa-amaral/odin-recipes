Hamburger


A classic hamburger is all about simplicity and balance: a juicy, well-seasoned beef patty served on a toasted bun with fresh toppings and your favorite condiments. This recipe keeps things easy while still delivering a flavorful burger with a crispy seared exterior and a tender, juicy center. Perfect for a quick lunch, dinner, or weekend cookout, this homemade hamburger can be customized with cheese, lettuce, tomato, pickles, onions, or sauces to match your taste.

  • 500 g (1 lb) ground beef (80/20 preferred for juiciness)
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 4 hamburger buns
  • 4 slices cheddar cheese (optional)
  • Lettuce leaves
  • 1 tomato, sliced
  • 1 small onion, sliced
  • Pickles (optional)
  • Ketchup, mustard, or mayonnaise

    • Steps

    1. Place the ground beef in a bowl and season it with salt, black pepper, and garlic powder. Mix gently until combined.
    2. Divide the beef into 4 equal portions and shape them into burger patties slightly larger than the buns.
    3. Heat a skillet or grill over medium-high heat and lightly oil the surface if needed.
    4. Cook the patties for about 3–4 minutes on the first side, then flip and cook another 3–4 minutes until desired doneness.
    5. Add cheese slices during the last minute of cooking so they melt over the patties.
    6. Toast the hamburger buns lightly on the grill or in the skillet for extra flavor.
    7. Assemble the burgers by placing the patties on the buns and adding lettuce, tomato, onion, pickles, and your favorite sauces.
    8. Serve immediately with fries, chips, or a side salad.
